Travel / overseas loading

Added to the applicable ordinary or overtime multiplier for travelling or overseas work. Example: 1.5x becomes 1.8x. Travelling entries remain capped at 10 paid hours and do not consume weekly overtime thresholds.

Allowances

Allowances are included in estimated gross: hourly applies to paid work and travel hours, weekly once per work week, and fixed once per work shift.

Weekly PAYG withholding

2026–27 ATO weekly Schedule 1 coefficients are preloaded. Add future financial years as new keys (for example, "2027-28") with scale1 and scale2 brackets. Each bracket has upper, a and b values; use null for the final upper limit. ATO weekly PAYG coefficients.

PAYG is estimated separately for each configured work week using the financial year containing that week's end date. Taxable gross includes earnings and allowances. This is not an EOFY assessment.
