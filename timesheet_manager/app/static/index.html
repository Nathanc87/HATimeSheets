Pay cycle

Normal working hours

Set the normal roster for each day. Work outside an enabled day hours is paid at a minimum of 1.5x, even before weekly overtime thresholds.

Public holiday credit

Default credit days are Monday–Thursday. The overtime treatment is deliberately left unconfigured until you confirm your agreement.

Overtime tiers

Each tier applies once accumulated weekly work hours reach its threshold. The highest reached tier applies; thresholds and rates are editable.

Travel / overseas loading

Added to the applicable ordinary or overtime multiplier for travelling or overseas work. Example: 1.5x becomes 1.8x. Travelling entries remain capped at 10 paid hours and do not consume weekly overtime thresholds.

Penalty rules

Day-of-week and public-holiday replacement/additional rules.

Allowances

Allowances are included in estimated gross: hourly applies to paid work and travel hours, weekly once per work week, and fixed once per work shift.

Estimated weekly income tax

Weekly PAYG uses the selected financial year's ATO Scale 1 or Scale 2 coefficients when available; these include the standard Medicare levy. Annual resident tax brackets are used only as a fallback when weekly coefficients are unavailable. HELP and Medicare levy surcharge are not included.
