Allowances

Hourly applies to paid work and travel hours, weekly once per work week, and fixed once per work shift.

Estimated weekly income tax

Weekly PAYG uses the selected financial year's ATO Scale 1 or Scale 2 coefficients when available; these include the standard Medicare levy. Annual resident tax brackets are used only as a fallback. HELP and Medicare levy surcharge are not included.

PAYG is estimated separately for each configured work week using the financial year containing that week's end date. Taxable gross includes earnings and allowances. This is not an EOFY assessment.

Normal working hours

Weekdays are fixed. Enable or disable each day and set its normal start and finish times. Work outside an enabled day's hours is paid at a minimum of 1.5×, even before weekly overtime thresholds.

Ordinary hours & overtime

Each tier applies once accumulated weekly work hours reach its threshold. The highest reached tier applies. Set the cost code name on the tier itself so the label and rate stay together.

Penalty rules

Day-of-week and public-holiday replacement or additional rules.

Other payroll labels

Public holiday and leave labels are managed separately from the overtime tiers.

Travel tiers & cost codes

Each travel tier owns its cost code name and multiplier. The calculated multiplier is still shown after the code in reports and exports.

Travel tier multipliers are the complete rates used for travel and overseas work. There is no separate additional loading. Travelling entries remain capped at 10 paid hours and do not consume weekly overtime thresholds.

Public holiday credit

Default credit days are Monday–Thursday. The overtime treatment is deliberately left unconfigured until you confirm your agreement.
